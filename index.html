I build systems that hold up in production.

I'm the kind of engineer who wants to know why something works, not just that it does. I like problems where the hard part is underneath — a race between native code and the web layer, a model that's confident and wrong, a pipeline that's fine until it meets real traffic. I'd rather ship something small that holds than something broad that almost works.

Most of my work sits between research and production: taking something that works in a notebook and making it survive contact with real users. I care about the seams — the error path, the slow query, the case nobody wrote a test for.
